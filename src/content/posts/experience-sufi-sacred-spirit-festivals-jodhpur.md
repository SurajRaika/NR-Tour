Jodhpur, the captivating Blue City of Rajasthan, is set to host two of the most soul-stirring cultural events in February 2026: the World Sufi Spirit Festival and the Sacred Spirit Festival. These festivals offer a unique opportunity to immerse yourself in ancient traditions, mesmerizing music, and the profound spirituality of India.

## World Sufi Spirit Festival (February 9-11 at Nagaur Fort, February 13-15 at Mehrangarh Fort)

Prepare to be enchanted by the World Sufi Spirit Festival, an annual gathering that brings together Sufi musicians, dancers, and artists from across the globe. Held against the breathtaking backdrops of Nagaur Fort and Mehrangarh Fort, this festival is a celebration of divine love, peace, and harmony. Experience soul-stirring qawwalis, whirling dervishes, and captivating performances that transcend cultural boundaries.

## Sacred Spirit Festival (February 13-15 at Mehrangarh Fort)

Following closely, the Sacred Spirit Festival at the majestic Mehrangarh Fort offers another deep dive into spiritual and traditional music. This festival showcases performances by renowned artists from Rajasthan and international acts, presenting a rich tapestry of sacred music, devotional poetry, and folk traditions. It's an experience that promises to elevate your spirit and connect you with the mystical heritage of the region.
